World Overview

  • Basic Information

    INC stands for Intermedia Network Center. It is a permanent public world on VRChat mainly designed for Japanese speakers, while also welcoming English-speaking visitors.

    The world is managed by the organizer and the INC production team, with a focus on calm and meaningful interaction among people interested in academia, technology, art, music, and culture.

  • Concept

    INC is intended for people with intellectual curiosity who enjoy deeper conversations. We hope the space feels welcoming to those with academic backgrounds or specialized interests, and approachable for international visitors who are interested in Japan.

  • About Group Participation

    Joining the VRChat group is optional. Group members may receive event updates, but group membership is not required to enter the world.
